## Full description

Comeback is a habit tracker with automatic step counting, built for getting back to your best one day at a time.

Set your daily targets, log them in a couple of taps, and watch your streak and charts grow. Miss a day? Every comeback has restarts. Start again today. No guilt, no nagging.

AUTOMATIC STEP COUNTER (PEDOMETER)
• Counts your steps in the background using your phone's own motion sensors. No other fitness app or account is needed.
• Smart vehicle filtering: it pauses while you are in a car, bus or on a motorbike or bicycle, and ignores bumps, shaking and short shuffles, so only real walking is counted.
• Estimated distance from your height. Choose Relaxed, Balanced or Strict filtering.
• Your total fills in by itself, and you can fix any day by hand. Edited days are never overwritten.
• A quiet notification shows today's count. A health row tells you if counting is paused and why, and brand-specific setup tips help it keep running on Xiaomi, Samsung, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Infinix, Tecno and more.

DAILY TARGETS AND RULES
• Targets for steps, brisk walking, pushups, pull-ups, squats, plank, water and sleep. Change any number, add your own, reorder or remove them.
• Simple rules you switch on when you keep them, like "no fried food" or "no sugary drinks".
• A progress ring, a score for the day and gentle messages when you hit your goals.

WORKOUT LOG, WEIGHT TRACKER AND NOTES
• Log workouts, weight, waist and a short note for each day.
• Fix yesterday or fill in a missed day at any time.

PROGRESS YOU CAN SEE
• Week, month and 3 month views, a touch-and-drag trend chart, a calendar of your days, average steps and distance, and steps by hour for today.
• Your current streak and best streak, shown kindly.

A DAILY ROUTINE THAT STICKS
• One gentle reminder a day at the time you choose. Turn it off any time.

CLOUD SYNC, BACKUP AND OFFLINE USE
• Works fully offline. Everything is saved on your phone first.
• Optional account: sign in with email to keep your log on all your phones. Each person's data is separate and private to their account.
• Automatic backup after every change, with 7 daily copies in Documents/Comeback. Export to JSON or a spreadsheet (CSV), and restore from a backup whenever you like.

PRIVACY
• Your habits, steps and notes stay on your phone unless you choose to sign in and sync.
• Steps are counted on the device from its motion sensors. They are not shared with other apps.
• Location is optional and off by default. If you switch it on, only your speed is used, on the phone, to tell when you are in a vehicle. Your position is never saved or uploaded.
• No ads.

Permissions: Physical activity (to read the step sensor), Notifications (for the daily reminder and the step count), optional Location, and an optional battery setting so counting is not stopped in the background.

Comeback is a habit and activity log. It does not give medical advice.

Get back to your best, one day at a time.
